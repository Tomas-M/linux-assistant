Payments

Open a user’s controls and choose Create payment link. Share the resulting Stripe Checkout URL yourself. A verified paid webhook adds the configured credit pack exactly once.

Configure STRIPE_SECRET_KEY, STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET, STRIPE_PRICE_ID and ASSISTANT_PACK_CREDITS in /etc/linux-assistant/server.env, then restart the service.

Stripe webhook: /webhooks/stripe · Snapshot events: checkout.session.completed, checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded.

PayPal is not integrated. For a payment received elsewhere, verify it in your payment provider, then add credits manually with its transaction reference as the reason. Refunds and disputes require a manual credit adjustment.
